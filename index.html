02 / Selected work

Problems, engineering decisions, and measurable outcomes.

01

Automation scale

Reducing a 39-hour regression cycle to 2 hours

Problem
A large API and integration suite had become too slow for practical release feedback.
Approach
Introduced parallel execution, execution-safe suite boundaries, and smoke/regression/nightly segmentation while preserving diagnostic evidence.
Outcome
Reduced end-to-end automated regression time by approximately 95% across a suite of more than 2,300 test cases.
02

Deterministic testing

Stabilizing external-provider scenarios

Problem
Financial-provider and coupon dependencies produced unstable data, timing, and failure behavior.
Approach
Used mock servers and service virtualization to control success, timeout, retry, invalid-response, and partial-failure conditions.
Outcome
Made critical failure-path validation repeatable and reduced dependence on unpredictable third-party environments.
03

Risk-based quality

Protecting high-impact financial journeys

Problem
Purchase, refund, settlement, and credit flows required stronger protection against duplicate or inconsistent financial effects.
Approach
Designed risk-based API and E2E coverage with negative paths, boundary conditions, state validation, and idempotency checks.
Outcome
Improved release confidence for high-risk workflows and made regression scope easier to prioritize and explain.

All case studies are intentionally generalized. Client identifiers, internal endpoints, credentials, production data, and proprietary implementation details are excluded.
